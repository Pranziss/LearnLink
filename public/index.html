Built for studying, not guessing

Answers with sources

Every answer points to the module it came from, so you can read the original and not just trust the reply.

Teacher approved

It answers from the materials your faculty publish first. If a topic isn't there, it says so, and any general answer is clearly labeled.

Runs on campus

Answers come from a BISU server on the campus network, so you can keep studying when the internet is down. No cloud AI, no API fees.

Made for phones

Ask from your phone or laptop over the campus Wi-Fi, and rate any answer that misses the mark.

How it works

  1. 1

    Teachers share modules

    Faculty keep posting in Google Classroom. LearnLink copies new posts in while the server is online, and the teacher publishes them. Files can also be uploaded on campus.

  2. 2

    You ask a question

    LearnLink matches your words by keywords and by meaning, then writes a short answer from the best passages.

  3. 3

    Check and rate

    Open the source to read the original, then tap helpful or not helpful.
