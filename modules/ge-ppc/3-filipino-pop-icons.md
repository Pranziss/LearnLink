# Module 3: Filipino Pop Icons

Filipino popular culture includes teleseryes, OPM, movies, comedy, and sports that many Filipinos follow together, and it often reflects family, humor, and resilience.

Both local and overseas Filipino audiences help spread Filipino pop culture through television, streaming, and social media.

- **Teleserye**: a Philippine television drama series, usually shown in the evening
- **OPM**: Original Pilipino Music, songs made by Filipino artists
- **Fandom**: a community of fans of a show, artist, or franchise
