# Module 2: Media and Society

Media shapes how people see the world by choosing which stories to tell and how to frame them. Audiences also shape media through what they watch, share, and ignore.

Media literacy is the ability to access, analyze, and question media messages, including spotting bias and misinformation.

- **Media literacy**: the skill of analyzing and questioning media messages
- **Stereotype**: an oversimplified idea about a group of people
- **Misinformation**: false information that spreads, even when the sharer does not mean to deceive
- **Framing**: the way a story is presented so that it highlights certain points
