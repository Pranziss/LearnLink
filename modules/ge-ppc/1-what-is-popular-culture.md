# Module 1: What Is Popular Culture?

Popular culture is the set of ideas, entertainment, and trends that are widely shared in a society, such as music, films, television, fashion, and online content.

It differs from high culture and folk culture because it is made for large audiences and spread through mass media.

- **Popular culture**: everyday entertainment and trends shared by large groups of people
- **Mass media**: channels such as TV, radio, newspapers, and the internet that reach large audiences
- **Trend**: a pattern of behavior or taste that spreads quickly
- **Subculture**: a group with its own interests and styles inside a larger culture
