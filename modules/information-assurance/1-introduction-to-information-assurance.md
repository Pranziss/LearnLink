# Module 1: Introduction to Information Assurance

Information assurance (IA) is the practice of protecting information and the systems that handle it so that it stays trustworthy, available, and used only as intended.

IA is broader than IT security. It covers people, policies, and processes as well as technology, and it applies to information in storage, in transit, and in use.

Organizations practice IA to reduce risk, meet legal duties such as the Philippine Data Privacy Act, and keep the trust of the people they serve.

- **Information assurance**: managing risks to information and the systems that use it
- **Asset**: anything of value to an organization, such as data, hardware, software, or people
- **Threat**: something that could cause harm to an asset
- **Vulnerability**: a weakness that a threat can exploit
