# Module 3: Risk Management

Risk is the chance that a threat exploits a vulnerability and causes harm. It is often described as likelihood multiplied by impact.

Risk assessment lists assets, identifies threats and vulnerabilities, estimates likelihood and impact, and ranks the risks so the most serious are handled first.

Once risks are ranked, an organization can accept, reduce, transfer, or avoid each one. Buying insurance is an example of transferring risk.

- **Risk**: the chance that a threat exploits a vulnerability and causes harm
- **Risk mitigation**: actions that lower the likelihood or impact of a risk
- **Residual risk**: the risk that remains after controls are applied
- **Control**: a safeguard or countermeasure that reduces risk
