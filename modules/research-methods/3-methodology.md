# Module 3: Methodology

The methodology explains how the study was carried out: the design, the respondents, the instrument, and the way data was gathered and analyzed.

Sampling picks a smaller group to represent the population. Probability sampling gives everyone a known chance of being chosen, while non-probability sampling does not.

- **Sampling**: selecting part of a population to study
- **Respondent**: a person who answers a survey or interview
- **Instrument**: the tool used to collect data, such as a questionnaire
- **Reliability**: whether a study gives consistent results when repeated
- **Validity**: whether an instrument measures what it is meant to measure
