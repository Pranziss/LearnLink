# Module 1: Research Basics

Research is a systematic way of finding answers to questions. Good research follows a clear process from identifying a problem to reporting the findings.

Quantitative research uses numbers and statistics, qualitative research explores meanings and experiences, and mixed methods combine both.

- **Research problem**: the issue or gap a study sets out to address
- **Variable**: a characteristic that can change or be measured
- **Hypothesis**: a testable prediction about the relationship between variables
- **Population**: the whole group a study is about
