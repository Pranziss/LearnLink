# Module 2: Review of Literature

A review of related literature shows what is already known about a topic. It helps you avoid repeating earlier work and reveals gaps your study can fill.

Use credible sources such as journal articles, theses, and books. Summarize and compare them in your own words, and cite every source to avoid plagiarism.

- **Research gap**: an area that earlier studies have not covered, covered only partly, or covered with weak evidence
- **Citation**: a reference that credits the source of an idea or fact
- **Plagiarism**: presenting someone else's work as your own
- **Primary source**: original material such as a survey, interview, or experiment
