# Module 1: Foundations of Ethics

Ethics is the study of what is right and wrong and how people ought to act. Morality refers to the beliefs a person or community holds about right and wrong, while ethics examines those beliefs and the reasons behind them.

Ethical reasoning means weighing facts, values, and the people affected before deciding. It lets us defend a decision with reasons, not only habit or opinion.

- **Morality**: the beliefs a person or community holds about right and wrong
- **Ethics**: the study of moral beliefs and the reasons behind them
- **Value**: something a person or group considers important
- **Moral dilemma**: a situation where every choice conflicts with some moral principle
