# Module 3: Professional Ethics

Professionals are expected to be honest, competent, and accountable, and to put the public's welfare ahead of personal gain. Many professions publish a code of ethics.

Conflicts of interest arise when a personal interest could influence professional judgment. They should be disclosed and managed.

- **Code of ethics**: a written set of standards a profession expects its members to follow
- **Conflict of interest**: a situation where personal interest could affect professional judgment
- **Accountability**: accepting responsibility for your actions and their results
- **Whistleblowing**: reporting wrongdoing inside an organization to protect others
