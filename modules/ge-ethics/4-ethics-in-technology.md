# Module 4: Ethics in Technology

Technology raises ethical questions about privacy, security, fairness, and the effect of automation on people and jobs. IT professionals often decide how much data to collect and how to protect it.

AI systems can repeat bias found in their training data, so developers should test for fairness and be open about limits.

- **Digital privacy**: a person's right to control how their data is collected and used
- **Algorithmic bias**: unfair results caused by biased data or design
- **Intellectual property**: creations of the mind that the law protects, such as software and writing
- **Digital divide**: the gap between people who have access to technology and those who do not
