# Module 2: Moral Theories

Utilitarianism judges an action by its results: the right action produces the greatest good for the greatest number of people.

Deontology says some actions are right or wrong regardless of results because of duty and rules. Virtue ethics focuses on character: what a good person would do.

- **Utilitarianism**: ethics that judges actions by the good they produce for most people
- **Deontology**: ethics based on duties and rules
- **Virtue ethics**: ethics based on developing good character traits
- **Categorical imperative**: Kant's rule to act only in ways you could want everyone to act
